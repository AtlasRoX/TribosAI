---
name: literature-claims
description: Literature reviews and questions about the state of research.
triggers:
  - \b(?:literature|review|state of the art|recent (?:results|work|advances)|survey)\b
  - \b(?:papers?|studies|preprints?)\b
---
1. Cite primary sources for each claim; prefer peer-reviewed papers and mark preprints as such.
2. Give years, and say when the newest source is old enough that the picture may have changed.
3. Where sources disagree, report both positions and what would decide between them.
4. Do not extrapolate a result beyond the setting the paper studied.
