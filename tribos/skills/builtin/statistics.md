---
name: statistics
description: Statistical inference and data analysis.
triggers:
  - \b(?:p[- ]value|confidence interval|regression|hypothesis test|t[- ]test|anova|significan\w*|standard error|bootstrap)\b
---
1. State the model and its assumptions (independence, distribution, equal variances) and whether the data support them.
2. Report effect sizes with confidence intervals, not p-values alone.
3. Say how many tests were run and whether multiple comparisons were corrected.
4. Distinguish association from causation explicitly.
